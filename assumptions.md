# Data Generation Assumptions

Patterns deliberately built into the synthetic data.
Retention queries must recover these, or the query is wrong.

## Overall targets

- First-year retention: ~78%
- Six-year graduation: ~55%

## Retention by major

| Major | First-year retention |
|---|---|
| Nursing | 85% |
| Business | 80% |
| Math | 70% |

## Predictors

- **Commuters** retain ~8 points below on-campus students
- **First-gen** students retain ~6 points lower
- High school GPA below 3.0 → roughly double the attrition rate

## Gateway courses

- MATH 2311 Calculus I — DFW rate 35%
- CHEM 2045 General Chemistry — DFW rate 30%
- Failing a gateway course roughly doubles attrition the following term

## Financial aid

- Students receiving institutional aid above $3,000/term retain ~10 points better
